# ChurchBell Demo Storyline — "From Notebooks to One Click"

## The Flow (45-60 min demo)

---

## ACT 1: THE STORY (5 min) — *Connect emotionally first*

**Open with your personal story.** This is your strongest weapon. No competitor has this.

> "I watched my own relatives — church treasurers — spend hours every Sunday after service, writing in multiple ledger books, cash books, day books, filling out receipt books by hand, and then stuffing receipts into subscription cards to send back to members. Every quarter, they'd spend days preparing reports for the PC Meeting. The annual report? Weeks of work.
>
> I'm a software engineer. God gave me this talent and I kept thinking — why can't we solve this? This became my dream from 2016. In 2021, during COVID, a church gave me the opportunity. I felt this was the calling. I built ChurchBell — not as a business, but as a service to the church."

**Then the growth line:**

> "We started with one church. Then two. Ran it for over a year. No ads, no campaigns — every church that joined came through word of mouth. That tells you something."

---

## ACT 2: THE PAIN → THE SOLUTION (30-35 min) — *Module by module*

The structure for each module: **"Today you do THIS manually... with ChurchBell, it's THIS."**

---

### 2A. PEOPLE — "Know your congregation" (10 min)

#### Members *(the foundation — demo this first)*

- **Pain:** "How long does it take you to find all members above 80 for senior citizen recognition? Or all kids under 12 for Sunday school? Flipping through registers?"
- **Demo:**
  - Show the member list → Apply filters (age, membership type, gender, area group) → Results in seconds
  - Show family management — family head linkage, family members view
  - Show member profile → transaction history right there → "A member walks up and asks 'How much have I paid this year?' — one click."
  - Show Excel import — "Your existing data in any Excel format, we import it. Day one, you're running."
  - Show export to Excel/PDF

#### Special Dates *(emotional moment)*

- **Pain:** "Every Sunday, someone has to manually check who has birthdays and anniversaries this week for the service prayer."
- **Demo:** Show the weekly view (Sunday to Saturday) → ready for the screen/slides
- **Wow moment:** "ChurchBell automatically sends birthday and anniversary wishes to members via WhatsApp every morning. And every Friday evening, the Presbyter gets a PDF with next week's special dates — automatically. No one has to do anything."

#### Groups

- Show Member Groups (Youth Fellowship, Women's Fellowship, Choir)
- Show Area Groups — "When the Presbyter does house visits or carol rounds, he can pull up all families in an area"

#### Committee

- Quick show of committee management with roles/positions

#### Member Reports

- Show filtered member report generation → PDF/Excel

---

### 2B. FINANCE — "The heart of church administration" (12-15 min)

#### Transactions *(this is where you win them)*

- **Pain:** "The treasurer writes in the cash book, the day book, the ledger, fills out a receipt, tears it from the book, puts it in the subscription card... for EVERY transaction. Multiply by 50 members on a Sunday."
- **Demo:** Create a receipt → Show how it auto-updates all books simultaneously
- **Wow moment #1:** "The moment I save this receipt, the member gets a WhatsApp message with the PDF receipt attached. AND an SMS confirmation. Instantly. No subscription card needed."
- Show the receipt PDF preview
- Show payment entry, contra entry
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Alt+Shift+T` → new receipt instantly. `Ctrl+S` to save. `Ctrl+Shift+S` to save and add another. Speed matters on a busy Sunday.

#### Service Register

- **Pain:** "A separate register book for every service entry"
- **Demo:** Show how service register entries link directly to transactions — one system, everything connected

#### Auction *(for churches that do this — it's a differentiator)*

- **Pain:** "During harvest festival, members bid on items. Some pay immediately, some over months. Tracking who owes what across multiple auctions — nightmarish in notebooks."
- **Demo:**
  - Create auction event → Add items → Assign to members
  - Show payment tracking → Show pending amounts per member across events
  - Bulk import auction items via Excel

#### Opening Balance

- Show bulk save, auto-calculate next month, cascading recalculation

#### Account Reports *(the quarterly/annual report killer)*

- **Pain:** "How many days does it take to prepare the PC Meeting report? The annual accounts?"
- **Demo:** Click through each report — all generated in seconds:
  - **Day Book** — chronological journal
  - **Receipts & Payments** — summarized
  - **Trial Balance** — with PDF export
  - **Account Statistics** — income vs expense breakdown
  - **Ledger Report** — per-ledger drill down

#### Monthly Defaulters

- "Select a month → instantly see who hasn't paid their subscription. No more checking card by card."

#### Statements

- Member-wise contribution statement → filter by ledger, date range → PDF/Excel
- "Audit-ready in seconds."

#### Subscription Insights

- Visual month-by-month grid showing each member's payment pattern — "You can see gaps at a glance"

## Demo Tips

1. **Use real-looking data** — Have a demo church with realistic names, transactions, and dates. Nothing kills a demo like "Test Member 1".
2. **Keep the WhatsApp receipt moment live** — If possible, send a real receipt during the demo so they see their phone buzz. That's the "wow" moment.
3. **Don't rush reports** — Linger on the report generation. Let them feel how fast it is vs. their current days-long process.
4. **Adapt to the audience** — If they don't do auctions, skip it. If they're a small church, emphasize the Rs. 3/member pricing. If they have old records, emphasize the Records module.
5. **End with the mobile app** — Leave them with the "future" vision in their hands.
