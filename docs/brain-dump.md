# ChurchBell Sales — Brain Dump

## The Problem (How Churches Work Today)

In the church, earlier everything was handled using manually ledger books, notebooks, and even receipts was written manually using a receipt books. Church treasurer will be writing in multiple ledgers for cash book, ledgers, day book, etc and also need to write receipts and keep it in the subscription card to be sent back to the members. And we will be spending hours to generate a report to be submitted every 3 months for PC Meeting and also for the year report we will be spending so much time.

Also, things like fetching the member list who are above 80 for senior citizen and for fetching the kids list, it was taking so much time. Now all the reports and the member list are fetched in seconds with a click of a button.

## Special Dates

For the special dates, in the church every week sunday service, we will be reading the birthday and anniversaries celebrating on that week from sunday to saturday and will be praying. In some churches, they will read out and in some churches, we will be presenting it in the slide in a big screen and in some churches, they will be sending greeting cards.

## Groups

In the groups, we have member groups and area groups. Member groups is for assigning groups like Mens Fellowship, Womens Fellowship, Youth Fellowship, Choir, etc. And area groups is for assigning area group which will be used by Presbyter during his house visit and for carol rounds.

## Service Register

Currently in the church we have a separate register book for writing down the service register entries. Service register entry is one per service. One advantage is that we have the link between service register and the transaction entry.

## Records

There are cases like the church having records in a notebook which is very old like 1950s where the pages are breaking. To maintain the records digitally, we have multiple modules in the records.

## Auction

This is also one of the core modules. Members will be buying different and multiple items during the auction and some will pay immediately and some will pay over multiple months. We need to track this and also have a way for the user to inform them about the pending amount they pay. Currently, it is little difficult to manage the tracking.

## Core Modules

For a church, the core modules are Member and Transactions. Auction comes next. And mainly the transaction reports are key during the audits. Sometimes a member will come and ask about their subscription payment history and it will take time for the church admin to fetch all those. Current scenario is a button click.

## WhatsApp & SMS Notifications

When we create a transaction, the receipt will be sent to the member in WhatsApp immediately — PDF attached and also an SMS also sent to the member.

## Mobile App (Planned / In Progress)

Member can view their details, update and also can see the transactions — amount they gave for subscription. And also the admin can add the transaction in the mobile app — view member details, transaction details, etc in the mobile app in the palm.

## Personal Story & Pricing Rationale

I saw my own relatives writing so many notebooks every Sunday after service so I thought why don't we create a software or something automation. God gave me the talent in programming and I want to give it back to something useful. This is my dream project from 2016. I got an opportunity from a church during the COVID. I felt this is the calling and I created this software as service and we started in one church and then expanded to another church and we were running this in two churches for more than a year. Then I thought this is a good software and can be useful for many churches so started expanding it. We didn't run any campaign or ads — everything is word of mouth. I created this as a service.

However, we are running this in cloud — everything is hosted in AWS and we need to pay for the cloud usage, file storing, external WhatsApp integration with Meta — all these are costs to us so we planned to come up with a minimum price which should be affordable by the big church and also the small church.

**Pricing: Rs. 3 per subscription paying member per month.**

We don't want to have hefty one time payment or one fixed payment for every month — which will be difficult for the small churches. We thought through many things and decided to charge what it really costs for us from AWS. There is no initial amount or initial setup costs.

## Setup Process

It will be a quick setup — church will be setup in an hour. Once the member data are ready — you can start using the application immediately. There can be a problem in importing the member data and that's why we brought an import functionality where you can provide the member details in an excel. The excel format can be your own format or we can provide our own format. Normally it will take a day or two max for member data import. We will be giving a two month trial period for you to get used to the application. We will be having an in person or online training session for you to get started.
